Party Setup & Cleanup · Sacramento

Party Setup & Cleanup from someone who's done it for 25+ years

Most Sacramento parties happen at home or in a backyard, and the host usually ends up doing the work: tables in the morning, trash all afternoon and dishes until midnight. Party setup and cleanup takes that off your plate.

Linda works birthdays, graduations, anniversaries, family reunions, baby showers and holiday gatherings all over the city, from compact Oak Park bungalows to big backyards in Natomas and the Pocket.

What's included

What we can take off your plate

  • Setting up tables, chairs and a serving area
  • Keeping trash, recycling and dishes under control during the party (on request)
  • Breaking down tables, chairs and decorations
  • Kitchen cleanup: dishes, counters, stovetop
  • Leaving the yard and living areas clean
  • Bagging trash and recycling for pickup

Examples

Typical party setup & cleanup jobs in Sacramento

A summer backyard birthday

Sacramento summers are hot, so we set up early, keep food and trash areas tidy through the heat of the day, and reset everything once the party winds down.

A graduation open house

Guests coming and going all afternoon means constant clearing. We keep the serving table and trash stations in order from start to finish.

A family reunion or holiday gathering

Big guest lists and lots of cooking. We keep the kitchen usable during the event and restore the house afterward.

How it works

Booking is one phone call

  1. Call Linda

    Tell her the date, the place in Sacramento and roughly how many guests, or what your home needs. She answers her own phone.

  2. Get a clear plan and price
