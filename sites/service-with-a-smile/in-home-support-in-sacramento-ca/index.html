In-Home Support · Sacramento

In-Home Support from someone who's done it for 25+ years

Many Sacramento families reach a point where a parent wants to stay in their own home but needs help keeping it up. Others are juggling work, kids or recovering from surgery and can't stay on top of the house. In-home support fills that gap.

Linda provides practical help around the home, not medical care. She keeps a steady routine, works at a respectful pace, and gets to know the household so each visit feels familiar rather than like a stranger showing up.

What's included

What we can take off your plate

  • Light housekeeping: kitchen, bathrooms, dusting and floors
  • Laundry, folding and changing bed linens
  • Tidying and organizing everyday living areas
  • Taking out trash and recycling
  • A regular schedule that suits the household
  • Keeping family members informed if they'd like

Examples

Typical in-home support jobs in Sacramento

Helping an older parent stay at home

Adult children often call on a parent's behalf. Linda keeps the home clean and comfortable and becomes a familiar, trusted face in the routine.

Support after a hospital stay or surgery

When someone is recovering and can't manage the housework for a while, short-term help keeps the home livable.

A regular hand for a busy family

Weekly or every-other-week visits so the house doesn't slide while everyone is working.

How it works

Booking is one phone call

  1. Call Linda

    Tell her the date, the place in Sacramento and roughly how many guests, or what your home needs. She answers her own phone.

  2. Get a clear plan and price

    Linda walks through what's included (setup, cleanup, dishes, trash, the venue's checklist) and gives you a price before anything is booked.

  3. We show up and do the work

    On the day, we arrive at the agreed time, set up or clean to plan, and check in with you as we go.

  4. Final walkthrough

    Before we leave, we walk the space with you or your venue contact so nothing is missed.

In-Home Support FAQs

Is this medical or personal care?

No. In-home support here means practical help around the house: cleaning, laundry and keeping things tidy. It isn't medical or nursing care.

How much does in-home support cost in Sacramento?

It depends on how often you'd like visits and what the household needs. Call (916) 866-2270 to talk it through and get a clear price.

Can I arrange this for my parent?

Yes. Many families call on behalf of a parent. Linda will talk with you and, if you'd like, with your parent, to set up a routine that works for them.
