West Sacramento, Yolo County

Hosting in West Sacramento: what to plan for

West Sacramento sits across the Sacramento River from downtown, in Yolo County rather than Sacramento County. It's a short drive over the bridges from Oak Park, which makes it one of the easiest places outside the city for us to reach.

The city mixes established neighborhoods like Broderick and Bryte with newer areas like Southport and the riverfront. Celebrations here often happen in homes and community spaces, and sometimes outdoors near the water.

Because it's so close, West Sacramento hosts get the same planning and the same person as our Sacramento clients: Linda plans the job, shows up and does the work.

Outdoor events near the river

Wind, heat and bugs by the water make outdoor setups harder. Tablecloths blow, trash attracts yellowjackets and food needs covering. We set up with that in mind and keep things cleared as the event goes.

Different city, different rules

West Sacramento has its own city services and trash pickup, separate from Sacramento's. We make sure trash and recycling are left the way your home or venue needs them.

Mixed older and newer homes

An older Broderick bungalow and a new Southport house call for different setups. We plan the layout around your actual space.

Smaller community rooms

Clubhouse and community rooms in West Sacramento's newer neighborhoods are often compact, with a small kitchen and strict rules. Tight spaces mean setup has to be planned around the layout, and cleanup has to be thorough to get the deposit back.
