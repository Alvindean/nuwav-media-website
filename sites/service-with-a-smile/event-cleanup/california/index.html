Overview

Event help across the Sacramento region

Service With A Smile works in the Sacramento region, the part of California we know best. Linda is based in Oak Park, and every job is planned and run by her personally, so we stay close to home rather than try to cover the whole state.

Event work around Sacramento follows the calendar. Spring and fall are the busiest months for weddings and quinceañeras. Summer brings backyard parties and reunions, often in 100-degree heat, which changes how food, trash and setup have to be handled. The holidays fill church halls and family homes.

Before you hire anyone

Three things worth checking

Ask exactly what's included

Setup only, cleanup only, or both? Dishes, trash removal, restrooms, table and chair breakdown? Get it in writing before the day.

Check the venue's rules first

Many halls and churches set a hard exit time and their own cleaning checklist. Share it with whoever you hire so nothing is missed and your deposit comes back.

Know who shows up

Ask who will actually be doing the work on the day. With us, Linda plans the job and is there for it.
