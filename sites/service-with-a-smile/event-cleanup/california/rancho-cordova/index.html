Rancho Cordova, Sacramento County

Hosting in Rancho Cordova: what to plan for

Rancho Cordova became a city in 2003 and sits east of Sacramento along US-50, about 20 minutes from our base in Oak Park. It mixes long-settled neighborhoods with newer developments to the south and east, and is home to many military and working families, a legacy of the old Mather Air Force Base.

That mix means a lot of household help as well as parties: busy families who want the house kept up, and relatives who want help for an older parent.

Rancho Cordova's newer neighborhoods to the south and east, like Anatolia and Sunridge, have clubhouse rooms and parks that host a lot of family events. The older areas near Folsom Boulevard tend toward home-hosted gatherings.

Busy, shift-working households

Many Rancho Cordova families work long or irregular hours. Regular housekeeping lifts a weekly job off their list.

Community hall rentals

Parties held in rented community and clubhouse rooms usually come with a cleaning checklist and a deposit on the line. We work from that checklist.

Heat on the east side

Summer afternoons here run hot. We set up outdoor events early and keep food and trash areas under control.

Keeping a home up alongside a busy schedule

Between work and family, the house often slips until guests are coming. A regular housekeeping routine means you're never starting from scratch before a party.
