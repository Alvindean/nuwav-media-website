What we do

Setup, cleanup and housekeeping, done by the owner

Service With A Smile sets up and cleans up family events: weddings, birthday and anniversary parties, reunions, quinceañeras, and Bar and Bat Mitzvahs. We also keep homes in order with regular housekeeping and in-home support.

Owner Linda D. Ray has done this work for more than 25 years. She plans each job with you herself and is there to make sure it gets done the way you agreed.
