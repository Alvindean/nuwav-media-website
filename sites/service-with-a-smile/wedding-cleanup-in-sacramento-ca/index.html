Wedding Setup & Cleanup · Sacramento

Wedding Setup & Cleanup from someone who's done it for 25+ years

Sacramento weddings range from church-hall receptions to backyard celebrations to rented event rooms, and a lot of them come with a catch: the family is expected to set up and hand the space back clean by a set time.

Linda has spent more than 25 years doing exactly that work. She sets up before the ceremony, keeps things tidy during the reception if you'd like, and clears the room at the end so your deposit comes back.

What's included

What we can take off your plate

  • Setting tables, chairs and linens to your layout
  • Arranging the serving and gift tables
  • Clearing plates and trash during the reception (on request)
  • Full breakdown: tables, chairs, décor removal
  • Kitchen and serving-area cleanup
  • Working from your venue's cleaning checklist

Typical wedding setup & cleanup jobs in Sacramento

A parish-hall reception with a 10 p.m. exit

We start clearing as soon as the last dances begin, so the room is ready to hand back on time.

How it works

Booking is one phone call

  1. Call Linda

    Tell her the date, the place in Sacramento and roughly how many guests, or what your home needs. She answers her own phone.

  2. Get a clear plan and price

    Linda walks through what's included (setup, cleanup, dishes, trash, the venue's checklist) and gives you a price before anything is booked.

  3. We show up and do the work

    On the day, we arrive at the agreed time, set up or clean to plan, and check in with you as we go.

  4. Final walkthrough

    Before we leave, we walk the space with you or your venue contact so nothing is missed.
