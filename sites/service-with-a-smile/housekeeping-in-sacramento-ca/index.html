Housekeeping · Sacramento

Housekeeping from someone who's done it for 25+ years

Housekeeping is where Service With A Smile started, and it's still the everyday heart of the business. Linda cleans homes across Sacramento, many of them older houses in Oak Park, Curtis Park and Land Park, where hardwood, original tile and small kitchens call for a careful hand.

Some clients want a one-time reset before guests or after a busy season. Others want a steady schedule. A growing number are families arranging help for an older parent who wants to stay in their own home.

What's included

What we can take off your plate

  • Kitchens: counters, sinks, stovetop, appliance fronts
  • Bathrooms: tubs, showers, toilets, sinks and mirrors
  • Dusting, vacuuming and mopping throughout
  • Beds made and linens changed on request
  • Trash and recycling taken out
  • Anything else, agreed with Linda before the first visit

Examples

Typical housekeeping jobs in Sacramento

Keeping up an older parent's home

Adult children often call on a parent's behalf. Linda keeps a familiar routine, works at a respectful pace, and keeps the family informed if they'd like.

Before the relatives arrive

A thorough one-time clean before a holiday or family visit, so you can focus on cooking and hosting.

After hosting a party

Pairs naturally with event cleanup. We reset the house the day after, even if we didn't work the party.

How it works

Booking is one phone call

  1. Call Linda

    Tell her the date, the place in Sacramento and roughly how many guests, or what your home needs. She answers her own phone.

  2. Get a clear plan and price

    Linda walks through what's included (setup, cleanup, dishes, trash, the venue's checklist) and gives you a price before anything is booked.

  3. We show up and do the work

    On the day, we arrive at the agreed time, set up or clean to plan, and check in with you as we go.

  4. Final walkthrough

    Before we leave, we walk the space with you or your venue contact so nothing is missed.
